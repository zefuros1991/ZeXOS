# Code of conduct

ZeXOS is a small, friendly project. Everyone is welcome here, whatever their experience with Linux.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are installing Linux for the first time.
- Assume good intentions, and say what is wrong without attacking the person.
- Stay on topic in issues and pull requests.
- Respect other people's time: share what you tried and what you saw, so a problem can be solved in fewer steps.

## What is not welcome

- Insults, harassment, or personal attacks
- Discrimination of any kind
- Sharing other people's private information
- Spam and advertising

## If something goes wrong

Comments that break these rules can be edited or removed, and people who keep breaking them can be blocked from the project.

To report a problem with someone's behaviour, use GitHub's private report: on the [Security tab](https://github.com/zefuros1991/ZeXOS/security) choose **Report a vulnerability** and say it is a conduct report. Only the maintainer can see it.
